Unmodified corresponding atlas data are supplied in assets/atlas-inputs/.
The corresponding surface-generation source is tools/build-region-surfaces.py.
The six assets/anatomy/aal3-*.glb files are modifications prepared 2026-09-30:
AAL3 label selection, anterior SMA restriction at MNI y > 0, marching cubes,
and mild Taubin smoothing. Full processing parameters and hashes are in
assets/region-surfaces.json. These derived assets and their generation script
are distributed under GPL-3.0-or-later, without warranty.

prepared assets retain their own licenses. This is reference anatomy, not
validated treatment-target geometry.
