NMAtlas v0.5 — corrections and verification
30 September 2026

75 findings from 31 sources. Updated in the existing application folder so the
local server remains at http://127.0.0.1:8773/ . No original PDFs or synced project
files were changed. The prior v0.4 distribution and a backup of edited files remain.

1. Finding-specific anatomy and laterality
Every finding now carries explicit per-entity hemisphere metadata. The renderer
uses those assignments rather than guessing a whole paper's side. Fitzsimmons
shows left DLPFC and left pre-SMA context. Nauczyciel stimulation is right-sided;
the PET-effect finding is bilateral. Figee FM016 and FM017 distinguish right NAc
from right lateral PFC and left medial PFC, respectively. FM018 preserves the left
NAc seed while keeping unspecified cortical laterality labeled as context.
Selecting a new finding resets a prior hemisphere filter so reported sides are
not silently hidden. Unknown laterality does not create a bilateral-effect claim.

2. Separate Tyagi networks
R2-19-1 now represents VC–medial OFC; R2-19-3 represents amSTN–lateral OFC/dACC/DLPFC.
Separate medial and lateral DKT orbitofrontal parcels are used. The existing
clinical-comparison finding remains R2-19-2. This is one additional finding from
the same paper, not an independent cohort. Whole STN and capsular masks remain
reference context, not reconstructed clinical subdivisions or patient tracts.

3. Pre-SMA versus dmPFC
Removed the identical whole-superior-frontal target highlights. Shared parcels
remain gray anatomical context; their displayed laterality follows each finding.
A separate, sourced scalp-landmark comparison distinguishes the more anterior
dmPFC approach (Dunlop, about 25% of nasion–inion distance) from the pre-SMA approach
reported by Mantovani (35%). Fitzsimmons' left-sided target is identified separately.
Dunlop's Talairach coil landmark is not plotted as an MNI coordinate; the published
MNI analysis seed keeps its distinct role. No target segmentation was invented.
Clicking shared reference anatomy offers target choices instead of picking the
first matching entity and silently switching from dmPFC to pre-SMA.

4. Evidence, functional maps and links
Selecting a study or finding chooses its linked available map or turns the overlay
off. A deliberately selected unrelated map is visibly labeled Comparison only,
including the selected paper's name. Depression TMS overlays are available in
Depression TMS and All evidence, not in OCD or depression DBS views. Shared URLs
preserve the chosen map (including Off), opacity, hemisphere, view preset, labels,
points, fibers and evidence selection. Arbitrary free-camera poses are not saved;
a free-view link restores a preset. Rotation updates the on-screen view label.

5. Viewing and coordinate controls
Medial presets isolate a hemisphere. Requesting Both from a medial view changes
to oblique so the filter and view remain compatible. Orientation axes rotate with
the camera. Point labels have anchored colored dots and connecting lines; collision
avoidance moves text without moving its true coordinate anchor. Published point
labels have priority over anatomical labels. Through-anatomy point projection is
explicitly disclosed in the scene and coordinate notes. Marker size is display-only.
Functional maps have a dedicated opacity slider. The cortex slider is disabled
while a map is active, with an explanation. Zero map opacity hides all mapped
cortex consistently, including highlighted parcels. Cortex opacity also scales
focused cortical parcels when no map is active.

6. Controlled depression DBS evidence
Added Holtzheimer 2017 (SCC; 90 randomized) and Dougherty 2015 (VC/VS; 30 participants).
Each card identifies its randomized sham-controlled design, controlled interval,
response definition, reported denominators, nonsignificant primary comparison,
source and interpretation limits. These additions were checked against published
abstracts; full text was not independently reviewed. Later open-label findings
are not substituted for the controlled result.

7. Other audit corrections
Object-valued sample sizes are formatted by cohort instead of [object Object].
Carmi's response card states 16/42 active and 5/45 sham, separately from the 99-person
ITT and 94-person modified ITT populations. Study design is visible on each card.

Verification
- 12 automated regression groups passed. These exercise the actual application
  selection/URL functions and shared display rules, plus all evidence references.
- Live browser checks confirmed Fitzsimmons' left-only context, Nauczyciel's
  bilateral-effect metadata, separate Tyagi networks, both new trial cards,
  Fox clearing Taylor, explicit comparison labeling, saved-map restoration,
  isolated medial view, zero map opacity, point leader lines, shared-parcel choices,
  and the free-rotation label. No browser errors or warnings were reported.
- All 32 original finding/limitation texts remain unchanged. The seven supplied
  Round 2 PDF hashes and both original public map hashes match the prior build.
- Fresh screenshots are in output/atlas-corrections-v0.5. The previous browser
  scripts/results are archived as historical v0.4 checks; they are not presented
  as a new exhaustive regression run.

Remaining spatial limitations
Precise pre-SMA/dmPFC target masks, participant-specific electrodes, stimulation
fields, response volumes and tractograms remain unavailable. Missing subdivisions
and pathways are still disclosed. No new nonlinear registration of mixed template
versions was performed, and cortical projections are not anatomically validated
individual targeting surfaces. These changes correct representation and controls;
they do not supply missing original 3D data or validate clinical targeting.
