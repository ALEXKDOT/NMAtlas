NMAtlas v0.7 — composable anatomical estimates
Reviewed and prepared 2026-09-30

DISPLAY
Seven region checkboxes are beside the fiber controls in every focus. Multiple
regions coexist, preserve the current camera and tract selection on toggling,
and persist across target/finding changes. Region opacity defaults to 0.16;
background cortex remains #859b82. Cortical shading uses the existing material
and lighting. The image legend identifies all checked colors and hidden states.
Shared VLPFC/OFC pars orbitalis carries spatial bands of both colors. Right OFC
uses the parent OFC blue. Broad regions have no newly created point markers or
centroid labels. Specific existing parcel labels and published point roles remain.

Functional maps pause region colors with an explicit message. They are restored
when the map is switched off. Shared URLs record all region selections, opacity
and tracts. Legacy region= links restore their region at the new 0.16 default.

BOUNDARY DEFINITIONS
DLPFC: CerebrA/DKT rostral + caudal middle-frontal parcels.
VLPFC: CerebrA/DKT pars opercularis + triangularis + orbitalis.
OFC: CerebrA/DKT medial + lateral OFC + pars orbitalis; overlap is explicit.
Right OFC: right-sided subset, not a different broad cortical region.
These are gross-anatomical approximations, not exact BA9/46, BA44/45/47 or
study-specific cortical stimulation masks. Existing upstream geometry is reused.

CerebrA source: Manera et al. 2020, Scientific Data 7:237.
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-020-0557-9
Prepared-mesh source and prior registration remain in assets/atlas.json.

New bilateral AAL3 surfaces:
- dmPFC: Frontal_Sup_Medial labels 19/20. This non-orbital medial superior-frontal
  proxy includes anterior medial cortex; its edges are not a unique functional
  or cytoarchitectonic dmPFC definition.
- Pre-SMA: Supp_Motor_Area labels 15/16, restricted to MNI y > 0 (anterior AC
  plane). The anterior-SMA anatomical rule follows Ji 2021 Methods 2.5.
  No patient-specific 15 mm superficial-distance restriction, inverse warping
  or connectivity-maximizing target is reproduced. Ji treated right pre-SMA;
  the optional overlay is bilateral anatomy, with sides filterable separately.
- sgACC: ACC_sub labels 151/152. Gray matter, not SCC white-matter DBS.

AAL3 source: Rolls et al. 2020, NeuroImage 206:116189.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116189
https://www.oxcns.org/aal3.html

PREPARATION AND LIMITS
Unmodified AAL3v1_1mm labels are bundled in assets/atlas-inputs. One-millimetre
sampling does not add precision to older AAL labels. Source laterality is retained,
including slight midline asymmetry. tools/build-region-surfaces.py selects labels,
uses marching cubes at 0.5, then four Taubin iterations (lambda .5, nu .53).
Maximum vertex movement is below 1.5 mm. There is no decimation or shape dilation.
Parameters, package versions, source affine, voxel counts, bounds and hashes are
recorded in assets/region-surfaces.json. Only the six additional meshes are built;
no clinical records or previous geometry are regenerated.

AAL3 MNI single-subject coordinates are placed by their source affine alongside
the existing MNI2009c background. No nonlinear inter-template registration was
performed or validated. Boundaries can mismatch the background and should be
read at regional, not millimetre precision. Anatomical estimates now replace the
v0.6 whole-superior-frontal/seed-only placeholders. The same bounded masks can
provide finding-specific anatomy with the reported side, without asserting a
participant-specific stimulation location or field.

All 76 finding/limitation texts remain unchanged. Supplemental anatomical sources
do not increase the clinical source count. VLPFC-amygdala sources retain their
mechanistic/experimental roles. The recalled bitemporofrontal/bifrontal name is
unresolved; right OFC is a documented possible match, not a confirmed identification.
