FUNCTIONAL BACKGROUND · DIXON 2017

What do these regions contribute?

An integrative review of overlapping roles in emotion. These proposed functions provide context for the clinical lessons.

Emotion regulation & regional names

Lateral PFC and emotion regulation

vmPFC is an umbrella term

Different authors include different combinations of sgACC, pgACC, medial OFC and rostromedial PFC. Read each study’s definition before treating these regions as equivalent.

dACC and aMCC

Dixon uses anterior midcingulate cortex (aMCC) for a territory often called dorsal ACC (dACC). It contributes to evaluating actions, their expected outcomes and costs. The atlas retains study-specific names and does not equate whole parcels with precise subdivisions.

Source & limits of this framework

The review combines human and animal evidence. Its proposed specializations vary in certainty: the sgACC account has limited human causal evidence, and the medial OFC account is explicitly a hypothesis. The review does not establish a TMS or DBS treatment mechanism.

Figures 2–5 and the associated text inform these comparisons; Figure 6 informs aMCC, and the lateral PFC and emotion-regulation sections inform regulation. See the paper’s limitations section. Figure arrows are conceptual anatomical relationships, not measured tract trajectories. No new 3D pathway or pgACC boundary is inferred.
