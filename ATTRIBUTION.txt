NMAtlas — 3D circuit atlas, v0.6, 30 September 2026

This project continues the user's local NMAtlas. It does not copy Omar Chishti's
source code, models, or visual assets; his White Matter Tract Explorer informed the
requested interaction style. https://github.com/omar-chishti/omar-chishti.github.io

1. HCP1065 POPULATION-AVERAGED TRACTOGRAPHY
Yeh FC. Population-based tract-to-region connectome of the human brain and its
hierarchical topology. Nature Communications 13, 4933 (2022).
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32595-4
https://brain.labsolver.org/hcp_trk_atlas.html
Source archive: https://github.com/data-others/atlas/releases/download/hcp1065/hcp1065_avg_tracts_trk.zip
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International.
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The HCP-derived coordinate assets in assets/tracts/ are provided under the same licence. Other sources added in v0.10 retain their own terms below.

Data were provided by the Human Connectome Project, WU-Minn Consortium
(Principal Investigators: David Van Essen and Kamil Ugurbil; 1U54MH091657), funded
by the 16 NIH Institutes and Centers that support the NIH Blueprint for
Neuroscience Research; and by the McDonnell Center for Systems Neuroscience
at Washington University.

Preparation: 11 original .trk.gz entries were retrieved by HTTP byte range from the
published archive, with ZIP CRC-32 verified. Each original file SHA-256 is recorded
in assets/atlas.json. NiBabel 5.3.2 interpreted TrackVis header orientation and
half-voxel conventions to produce RAS+ millimetre coordinates. Up to 550
streamlines per file were sampled at evenly spaced indices, then resampled by
arc length at approximately 1.8 mm, capped at 100 points per streamline. No
registration, anatomical editing, endpoint labeling or patient-specific inference
was added. The webpage applies one display rotation: RAS [x,y,z] -> [x,z,-y].
Colors identify bundles; display density is not an anatomical measurement.

2. PREPARED ANATOMICAL MESHES
Batuhan Ayci. Clinical Neuroanatomy Atlas, public data release v1.0.3.
https://github.com/aycibatuhan/nervous-system-atlas/releases/tag/v1.0.3
Bundle: atlas-data-v1.0.3.tar.gz
SHA-256: 728a29ab97bfd0703dc1e01769c6d48ac34287500d1bb79544d8f6f858423db7
Selected prepared geometry is reused without coordinate changes. Runtime materials,
colors, opacity and visibility are changed. The bundle's generated data is offered
under CC BY-SA 4.0, with the original datasets retaining their own upstream terms.
The original source metadata, transformation labels and mesh hashes are retained
in assets/atlas.json. Licence texts are included in assets/licenses/.

Principal upstream sources used:
- MNI152NLin2009cAsym template, (c) Louis Collins, McGill University; Fonov et al.
  TemplateFlow distribution, MNI/McGill permissive template terms.

  and provenance caveat are retained in the MNI-FreeSurfer licence record.
- MASSP multi-contrast subcortical parcellation, Pierre-Louis Bazin and colleagues,
  University of Amsterdam; eLife 2020. Apache 2.0 (author atlas-prior terms; the

  Scientific Data 2020; CC BY 4.0. Supplies BNST geometry in the prepared bundle.

  https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-020-0557-9. CC0 source labels; prepared meshes

Alignment: HCP tracts use ICBM 2009a Nonlinear Asymmetric; the prepared mesh bundle
reports MNI152NLin2009cAsym with source-specific transformation metadata.
No new 2009a-to-2009c nonlinear registration was performed. The shared RAS-mm
coordinate overlay is for reference visualization and has not been validated for
millimetre-level target-to-tract measurements. Native coordinates are preserved.

PUBLIC FUNCTIONAL MAPS
Two public NeuroVault volumes uploaded by siddiqis, collection 13075, downloaded
30 September 2026. CC0-1.0 under https://neurovault.org/FAQ, question 13.
- Image 787858: Dysphoric-anxiosomatic targeting atlas, Siddiqi et al., AJP 2020,
  Figure 5. DOI 10.1176/appi.ajp.2019.19090915.
  https://neurovault.org/images/787858/
  Positive: hypothesized anxiosomatic preference. Negative: dysphoric preference.
  Repository space: GenericMNI; exact template version unspecified.
- Image 787859: Convergent causal depression map, Siddiqi et al., Nature Human
  Behaviour 2021, Figure 5a. DOI 10.1038/s41562-021-01161-1.
  https://neurovault.org/images/787859/
  This public group t map is referenced in Taylor 2026, Figure 2 and Methods.
  It is not a participant-specific R map or an individualized treatment target.
  Repository space: MNI152NLin2009cAsym. Native 2-mm grid and affine retained.
The native compressed NIfTIs and exact checksums are included. Browser arrays are
little-endian float32, C order, with the original voxel-to-world transforms.
Values are trilinearly sampled onto the reference cortical surfaces in shared
MNI coordinates; no new nonlinear registration is performed. Deep voxel values
are not represented on cortex. The display scale clips at the 95th percentile
of absolute nonzero finite values; this is not a significance threshold.
Color sign is defined separately for each map and is not excitation/inhibition.
No newly colored region is claimed to be a validated or accessible TMS target.

PUBLISHED POINT MARKERS
Five MNI coordinates are transcribed with source locators and original template
qualifications. Fox Figure 3 supplies two candidate left-DLPFC targets; Fox Table
1A, Morriss Methods and Dunlop Methods supply three analysis seeds. Point radius
is a visibility aid, not an ROI radius, electric-field size or treatment tolerance.
No Talairach point was silently converted to MNI. No patient target was inferred.

8. AAL3 anatomical estimates added in v0.7
Rolls ET, Huang CC, Lin CP, Feng J, Joliot M. Automated anatomical labelling
atlas 3. NeuroImage 2020;206:116189. doi:10.1016/j.neuroimage.2019.116189.
Author labels: https://www.oxcns.org/AAL3v1_for_SPM12.zip
Six derived surfaces represent medial superior frontal anatomy (dmPFC proxy),
anterior SMA (pre-SMA proxy; y > 0), and subgenual ACC. These retain source MNI
affine placement without new registration to the background. The pre-SMA rule
follows the anatomical part of Ji et al. 2021, doi:10.1002/hbm.25468; no individual
connectivity target or superficial-distance restriction is reproduced.
Source labels and generation script are included; hashes and processing details
are in assets/region-surfaces.json. License notice: assets/licenses/AAL3-notice.txt
and assets/licenses/GPL-3.0.txt. No original papers are redistributed.
