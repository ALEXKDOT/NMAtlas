# Regional functions · v0.15.0

Source: Dixon ML, Thiruchselvam R, Todd R, Christoff K (2017). Emotion and the
prefrontal cortex: An integrative review. Psychological Bulletin 143(10):1033–1081.
DOI: 10.1037/bul0000096. PMID: 28616997.

## Review scope

Reviewed the supplied 49-page author proof, its main text and all 11 figures,
and the four separately supplied color extracts (Figures 2–5). Those images
belong to the same review and are not independent evidence. Published metadata
was verified against PubMed. Proof page numbers below refer to the supplied
proof, not the final journal pagination. No original files are redistributed.

## Display and evidence boundaries

- Dixon is a supplemental background source in the circuit catalog. It adds no
  outcome records: evidence.json remains 94 findings / 39 sources.
- Teaching region details and the embedded anatomy share their descriptions and
  Dixon citation. sgACC and OFC atlas hover cards include the same background.
- pgACC appears only in the conceptual comparison. A broad rostral ACC parcel is
  explicitly not an exact pgACC boundary; no new node drives 3D geometry.
- All six clinical lessons, their steps, clinical links, Tyagi outcome claims,
  Seguin routes, hemisphere mappings and existing reference geometry are retained.
- Conceptual anatomy, functional relationships and treatment evidence remain
  distinct. No direct DLPFC–sgACC fibers or treatment mechanism are inferred.
- The source stays editable locally; the public build retains shared-password
  encryption and excludes the original PDF and separate supplied figure images.
