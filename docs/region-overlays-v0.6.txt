NMAtlas v0.6 — regional overlay update — 30 September 2026

REQUEST AND REPRESENTATIONS
The Anatomy overlay control is independent of the clinical target and evidence
list. It replaces the anatomy highlight, clears functional color maps and shows
its own source/role/limitations. Selecting a finding, study or target returns to
source-linked anatomy. Color, opacity and size do not imply treatment efficacy.

DLPFC: existing broad middle-frontal parcels, now directly selectable by region.
OFC: existing medial/lateral orbitofrontal and orbital IFG reference parcels.
Right OFC: a right-only version with Nauczyciel 2014 as supporting OCD TMS evidence.
VLPFC: broad opercular, triangular and orbital IFG context. Pars orbitalis was
added to the broad VLPFC mapping; it is relevant to anterior/ventral VLPFC circuit
literature, but these parcels are not the published connectivity endpoints.

LIMITS THAT REMAIN UNRESOLVED
- dmPFC: shared superior-frontal context and Dunlop 2016's MNI analysis seed only.
  The Talairach scalp/coil landmark is not plotted as MNI or as cortex.
- Pre-SMA: shared superior-frontal context only. A validated target boundary and
  common stimulation point are unavailable. Ji 2021 used individually selected
  pre-SMA sites; its STN seed is not a pre-SMA treatment coordinate.
- sgACC: Fox 2012's MNI analysis seed only. No precise gray-matter mask or SCC
  white-matter DBS geometry is supplied. Its right-sided centroid is not a claim
  that all sgACC/SCC treatment targets are unilateral.
- No individual coil locations, electric fields, patient tractograms, measured
  connectivity-strength overlays or validated new registrations were created.

TERMINOLOGY
“Subglossal cingulate” is interpreted as subgenual cingulate (sgACC). The existing
subcallosal white-matter DBS view remains separate.
“Right bitemporofrontal/bifrontal cortex” could not be identified confidently.
Right OFC is a plausible match and independently supported OCD target; the
uncertain phrase has not been converted into an anatomical structure.

LITERATURE AND REVIEW DEPTH
Existing source-linked findings cover Fox, Nauczyciel, Mantovani, Fitzsimmons,
Dunlop 2016, Tyagi and OCD DBS VLPFC connections.
Dunlop 2020 (PMID 31711880; doi:10.1016/j.brs.2019.10.020) adds one negative
controlled depression TMS outcome card. Published abstract reviewed; no new
spatial data obtained. All 75 previous finding/limitation texts are retained.
Sydnor 2022 (PMID 35731882; doi:10.1126/sciadv.abn5803) supports the mechanistic
VLPFC–amygdala rationale in healthy participants, not depression/OCD efficacy.
Oathes 2023 (doi:10.1038/s44220-023-00165-2) provides depression circuit-probing
context; the VLPFC site is an experimental comparator, not a demonstrated
therapeutic target. Ji 2021 (doi:10.1002/hbm.25468) informs pre-SMA targeting limits.
For these supplemental references, relevant abstracts/indexed primary-paper
methods/results were reviewed; unrestricted direct full-text retrieval was not
available for every paper. Precise review roles are stored per citation.
Nauczyciel's full publisher text was checked again, including the Fp2 scalp
landmark and separation of right stimulation from bilateral metabolic effects.
